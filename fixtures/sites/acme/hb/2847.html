How we hire engineers

Our loop is four stages and we aim to finish it inside two weeks. We do not run whiteboard algorithm interviews.

  1. Intro call, 30 minutes. A conversation with the hiring manager about what you have built and what you want next.
  2. Take-home, timeboxed to three hours. A small routing problem against a real dataset. We pay for your time.
  3. Take-home review, 60 minutes. We read your code together and ask what you would change with another day.
  4. System design, 60 minutes. We design a dispatch service under realistic constraints. We care about how you reason about failure, not whether you recall a specific technology.

We share the questions in advance for stage four. Surprise is not a signal we find useful.
